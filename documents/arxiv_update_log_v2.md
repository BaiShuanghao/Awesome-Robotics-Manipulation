# Revision v2 Update Log

This document summarizes the major updates introduced in Revision v2 of **_Towards a Unified Understanding of Robot Manipulation: A Comprehensive Survey_**.  
The revision focuses on correcting typographical, citation, and factual errors; refining the taxonomy and organization of the survey; expanding literature coverage; and updating figures, tables, and bibliography metadata.

---

## 1. Typo and Language Corrections

- Corrected spelling, capitalization, and terminology inconsistencies throughout the manuscript.
- Representative examples include:
  - Corrected the category label in **Figure 1** from *Dexterous Hand* to *Soft Hand*.
  - Corrected **MINE** to **MIME** in **Table 4**.
  - Corrected **AI4Sicence** to **AI4Science** in **Figure 24**.
- Revised several sentences to improve clarity, consistency, grammatical correctness, and academic readability.

---

## 2. Citation and Reference Corrections

- Corrected several mismatched citations across the main text, figures, tables, and bibliography.
- Representative examples include:
  - Corrected the **ROSIE** citation in the data-augmentation taxonomy to ensure consistency with the main text.
  - Corrected the citation associated with **3D FlowMatch Actor**.
  - Corrected the citations and naming of **HumanVLA** and **Humanoid-VLA**.
  - Corrected malformed venue/year information for **OmniVLA**.
- Normalized several bibliography entries whose metadata had been inconsistently imported from arXiv, conference websites, or other sources.

---

## 3. Content and Factual Corrections

- Corrected factual inaccuracies in benchmark and dataset statistics.
- Representative examples include:
  - Updated the number of objects in **GENMANIP** in **Table 2** from **10** to **10K**.
- Revised several method descriptions to more accurately reflect their original formulations, training procedures, and experimental settings.

---

## 4. Taxonomy and Structural Updates

- Clarified the distinction among:
  - **High-level Planning**
  - **Low-level Action Modeling**
  - **Actuation-level Control**

- Refined the overall organization of the robot-manipulation pipeline and the corresponding methodological taxonomy.

- Expanded the resource taxonomy in **Section 3** from five to **six** major categories by adding:
  - **Human Video Datasets and Video-World-Model Benchmarks**

- Added dedicated manipulation-task sections for:
  - **Aerial Manipulation**
  - **Underwater Manipulation**

- Expanded the discussion of major challenges and bottlenecks with dedicated sections on:
  - **Agent**
  - **Human-Robot Interaction and Collaboration**

- Added and refined four prospective future research directions.

---

## 5. Literature Coverage Updates

- Substantially expanded the literature coverage across nearly all major sections of the survey.
- Added a large number of **recently accepted and newly published papers** to better reflect the latest developments in robot manipulation.
- Increased the total number of references from **1.2K+ to 1.4K+**.
